# The rest of the icons

Everything the app still draws, beyond the four library chips, in the same
hyper-real audio-object language: **the unit is the idea.** What physical thing
could actually be this?

## Not included, and why

`curator-brain`, `curator-loop`, `curator-crystal`, `curator-hand` and
`curator-mixtape` are **no longer drawn**. They used to be the face of every AI
and curated mix, but since the album mosaic landed `MixCover` reads only
`art.hue` from them and never `art.src` — they are hue lookups now. Five pieces
of existing art are effectively retired; nothing here replaces them because
nothing renders them.

## Append to every prompt

> Hyper-realistic 3D product render. Studio lighting, shallow depth of field,
> physically based materials, real reflections and refraction — photographic,
> not illustrated. One object, centred, on a transparent background. No scene,
> no ground plane, no cast shadow, no backdrop. Bright enough to read on a
> near-black surface. No text, no letters, no numerals, no logos.

## One thing that had to be fixed by hand

These were written by five agents working in parallel, which meant none could
see the others' choices — and three objects were picked twice: a ribbon
microphone for both Jazz and R&B, a spring reverb tank for both Ambient and
Chill, a pocket cassette player for both J-pop and the 1980s. **Those tiles sit
in one grid**, so a repeat reads as a bug rather than a coincidence. The three
were regenerated against the full list of claimed objects. Worth knowing if this
set is ever extended: check the whole grid for repeats, because the writer of
any one tile cannot.

## Moods

## Recent and New Playlist

These are **controls**, not destinations: one opens a view, one opens a dialog,
and they sit in a grid beside playlist tiles showing real album art. A previous
attempt at giving them faces was reverted for exactly that reason — an object
here competes with the records around it. So these are briefed for restraint and
ordered **quietest first**. The label sits outside the tile, so the face is free,
but the object is drawn at only ~68% of it.


### Recent


**1. Parked tonearm rest**


> A short brushed-aluminium tonearm and headshell lowered into its chromed rest clip on a small machined pillar, just back from a side. Satin metal tube, black rubber cue damper, a slim counterweight at the tail, the finger-lift catching one cool highlight. Nothing lit, nothing printed on any surface. One calm diagonal, compact, generous empty margin around it, silhouette simple enough to read at thumbnail size.


**2. VU peak-hold flag**


> A small squat VU meter with a completely blank ivory face, no scale and no markings of any kind, lit from behind by a warm filament lamp to a soft creamy glow. The slim black needle has fallen back toward rest after a passage, while a thin amber peak-hold flag still sits parked out to the right, holding what just happened. Brushed bezel, domed glass, everything else matte and still.


**3. Fading oscilloscope trace**


> A small round oscilloscope tube seen face-on, its phosphor screen still carrying the decaying green trace of a waveform that already stopped, bright at one end and fading to nothing along its length. Plain glass with no graticule and no markings, a modest nickel bezel, the dark glass shoulder of the tube receding behind. Only the afterglow is lit; the rest of the object stays quiet and matte.
